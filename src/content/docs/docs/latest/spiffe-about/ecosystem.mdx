SPIFFE is supported by a broad ecosystem: systems that issue SPIFFE identities to workloads, the many services that accept those identities in place of passwords and API keys, and the libraries and tools that connect the two.

Each entry is marked as a **SPIFFE project**, **open source**, or **commercial**. Systems that accept SPIFFE identities show which SVID formats they accept, and whether they are **SPIFFE-native** (they have SPIFFE-specific support) or **SPIFFE-compatible** (they authorize by SPIFFE ID through a general X.509 or JWT mechanism).

This list is not exhaustive. To add or correct an entry, open a [pull request to this site](https://github.com/spiffe/spiffe.io).

## SPIFFE issuers

Systems whose main purpose is issuing SPIFFE identities to workloads. The [SPIFFE overview](/docs/latest/spiffe-about/overview/#spiffe-issuers) compares the capabilities they support. Some systems listed under other headings, such as service meshes, also issue SVIDs and are marked **Issues SVIDs**.
