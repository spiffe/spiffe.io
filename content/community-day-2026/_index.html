In 2026, SPIFFE turns ten. To mark a decade of open, vendor-neutral workload identity, we're holding a SPIFFE Community Day - a chance to bring the community together, celebrate how far the project has come, and help shape where it goes next.

We'll be hosted by Bloomberg in the City of London, with the event also streamed online via Zoom so you can join from wherever you are.

The afternoon mixes talks from teams running SPIFFE and SPIRE in production, updates from the project maintainers, and more exploratory topics on where workload identity is heading, from AI agents to authorization. Expect plenty of room to learn from - and talk to - the people building and using these projects.
