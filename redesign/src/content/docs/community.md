## Community Day 2026

[See the agenda and registration](/community-day-2026/) for 27 October in London and online.

## Join the conversation

Ask implementation questions, discuss a proposal, or share what you have built in [SPIFFE Slack](https://slack.spiffe.io/).

The community organizes its technical work through [Special Interest Groups and Working Groups](https://github.com/spiffe/spiffe/tree/main/community). Their scopes and meeting information live with the project.

## Make a contribution

A useful contribution can be a documentation correction, an integration, or a report of where a specification is unclear.

| Work on | Start here |
| --- | --- |
| The standard | [SPIFFE contribution guide](https://github.com/spiffe/spiffe/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) |
| The implementation | [SPIRE contribution guide](https://github.com/spiffe/spire/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) |
| The website | [spiffe/spiffe.io](https://github.com/spiffe/spiffe.io) |
| The ecosystem | [Add or update a project](https://github.com/spiffe/spiffe.io/blob/master/redesign/src/data/ecosystem.yaml) |

Discuss significant design changes with the relevant group before implementing them.
